Sampled 13 Nov 2026: housebets.com appears in the top 5 for none of "housebets", "housebets bonus", "housebets login", "housebets review"… Affiliates and lookalike domains (housebets.co, housebets.me.uk, housebetcasino.com) answer instead — including the login query, a phishing-shaped hole.

The facts contradict each other everywhere

Four incompatible welcome-bonus stories (none / $1,000 code MAX / $500 + spins / "$25,000 at 1x"), four launch years, and a scored review claiming there is no sportsbook. AI answer engines inherit all of it — the official site gives them nothing to cite.

4

Its best claims are unowned

"Instant withdrawals" is Housebets' own headline — and its meta description — yet payout-speed answers never mention it. Provably fair is a product feature with no page. The promotions page states no terms a crawler can quote.
